## Abundance

### Make Hidden Access Visible.

Abundance is an opportunity intelligence system exploring a deceptively simple question:

> **Before someone spends their own resources, has another system already created legitimate funding, access, generosity, sponsorship, subsidy, or unused capacity for what they need?**

The concept moves beyond a traditional resource directory toward a system capable of discovering, verifying, explaining, matching, and helping people act on fragmented opportunities.

The larger thesis is that resources frequently exist before people know how to find them. Scholarships, workforce funding, public programs, nonprofit capacity, employer benefits, corporate credits, sponsorships, donated resources, and community support often remain hidden behind fragmented systems and unfamiliar language.

Abundance explores what happens when those systems become legible.

**Status:** Public Product Prototype  
**Domain:** Opportunity Intelligence · Access · Public Interest Technology · AI

## Alive Athlete OS

### Train for Range. Not One Identity.

Alive Athlete OS is a product and research system exploring **multifaceted athleticism**.

Rather than optimizing a person around one performance metric or athletic identity, the system explores broad physical capability through the RANGE-9 model.

### RANGE-9

1. Strength
2. Aerobic Engine
3. Power + Speed
4. Mobility
5. Balance + Stability
6. Coordination + Agility
7. Impact Resilience
8. Recovery + Regulation
9. Play + Skill

The system is being developed around an explainable human decision loop:

> **Map → Choose a Season → Dose → Check State → Explain & Rebalance → Observe → Retest → Learn**

The athlete remains the final decision-maker.

Recommendations are intended to be explainable, contestable, adjustable, and responsive to lived evidence rather than presented as opaque universal scores.

The current research direction intentionally prioritizes proving the decision loop before adding wearables, automation, or complex hardware integrations.

**Status:** Private Research & Product Development  
**Domain:** Human Performance · Health · Athletic Intelligence · Research

## Context Assurance

Context Assurance is a developing socio-technical research framework focused on a question that becomes increasingly important as AI systems become more personalized:

> **Can the human context influencing an AI-assisted decision actually be trusted?**

AI systems increasingly rely on information about people: behavior, preferences, health, history, environment, identity, goals, patterns, constraints, and lived experience.

But contextual information is not automatically reliable simply because it exists.

Context Assurance explores whether task-relevant context has sufficient:

- Authority
- Provenance
- Freshness
- Relevance
- Integrity
- Uncertainty awareness
- Minimization
- Contestability
- Human review
- Appropriate scope

before it influences consequential decisions.

The initial research direction examines AI-assisted health systems, where incomplete or incorrectly interpreted human context can materially alter the quality of an output.

The broader thesis extends beyond health into any environment where AI attempts to understand humans deeply enough to influence consequential decisions.

**Status:** Private Research Program  
**Domain:** AI · Health · Context · Governance · Human Agency

# How I Build

I am particularly interested in systems that are:

## Human-Owned

Technology should extend human capacity without quietly replacing human agency.

People should retain meaningful control over consequential decisions affecting their lives.

---

## Context-Aware

Information without context can produce confident but deeply incomplete conclusions.

Systems should recognize that meaning changes according to environment, history, goals, constraints, relationships, timing, and lived reality.

---

## Explainable

People should be able to understand why a system produced a consequential recommendation.

Complexity is not an excuse for opacity.

---

## Contestable

Humans need meaningful ways to challenge, correct, defer, modify, or reject system outputs.

A system that cannot be questioned should not quietly govern consequential human decisions.

---

## Evidence-Aware

A system should know the difference between:

- Evidence
- Interpretation
- Inference
- Assumption
- Correlation
- Uncertainty
- Unknown information

Those categories should not silently collapse into one another.

---

## Interdisciplinary

The most consequential problems rarely respect professional boundaries.

Health problems can be technological.

Technology problems can be behavioral.

Behavior problems can be environmental.

Organizational problems can be structural.

Cybersecurity problems can be governance problems.

Access problems can be information problems.

Solving complex systems often requires thinking across disciplines rather than defending disciplinary boundaries.

---

## Alive

Systems ultimately exist in service of people — not the other way around.

Efficiency is not always the highest human value.

Optimization is not always improvement.

A technically successful system can still fail the humans living inside it.

# The Thread Across My Work

My work continually returns to several recurring relationships:

### Information → Meaning

Raw information is rarely enough.

Context determines what information means.

---

### Meaning → Decision

Knowing something does not automatically tell an institution what to do next.

Decision architecture matters.

---

### Decision → Accountability

Consequential decisions require visible ownership.

Accountability cannot disappear inside automation.

---

### Technology → Human Agency

The more capable technology becomes, the more deliberately we must decide where humans remain responsible.

---

### Complexity → Legibility

Complex systems do not always need to be simplified.

Sometimes they need to be **made legible**.

---

### Fragmentation → Coherence

Many difficult problems are not caused by a complete absence of information.

They are caused by information living in disconnected places.

---

### Visibility → Access

Resources, opportunities, capabilities, and human potential can exist without being visible to the systems responsible for recognizing them.

Making hidden systems visible changes what becomes possible.
